# E-commerce application

E-commerce often has less human friction between ad and purchase, but substantial information and trust friction. Product, price, delivery, returns, variants and checkout are parts of the same argument.

## Stage-specific e-commerce playbook

| Stage | Primary creative | Destination | Important downstream signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Lifestyle story, use case or qualified pattern interrupt | Advertorial, inspiration or problem-led product page | Engaged view → product view |
| Problem Aware | Symptom, diagnosis and pain-to-hope | Problem → solution principle → relevant product | Product interest and returning users |
| Solution Aware | Category comparison and buying criteria | Category or product page with demo and criteria | ATC, comparison and branded search |
| Product Aware | UGC review, raw demo, objection and proof | Product page with real-life visuals, reviews and FAQ | ATC, checkout and new-customer CVR |
| Most Aware | Price, offer, real deadline, cart or stock | Variant, cart or checkout | Purchase, contribution margin and AOV |

## Product imagery and UGC by stage

- Unaware: show the situation and desired experience before product detail.
- Problem Aware: show the product resolving the diagnosed friction, not merely existing in frame.
- Solution Aware: demonstrate the decision criterion side by side or step by step.
- Product Aware: show the actual SKU, size, fit, sound, finish, material and use case without excessive polish.
- Most Aware: make variant, price, delivery and CTA dominant.
