# Lead-generation application

In a lead business, the form is not the sale. It transfers the prospect into another part of the decision. Quality, contact, qualification, booking, attendance, proposal, deposit and revenue form one chain.

## Stage-specific lead playbook

| Stage | Best ask | Form or page | Sales hand-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | View guide, inspiration or quiz | Very low friction | Nurture; avoid immediate hard sell |
| Problem Aware | Receive an assessment or explanation | Short needs clarification | Diagnostic conversation |
| Solution Aware | Get a recommendation, estimate or demo | Qualifying fields | Advise on criteria and fit |
| Product Aware | Book consultation or see detailed case | Price, process, proof and expectations | Resolve the remaining objection |
| Most Aware | Choose time or pay deposit | Calendar or high-intent form | Confirm and reduce no-show risk |

## Lead quality over cheapest CPL

- Match form friction to awareness: low friction for learning; more qualification near the sale.
- A cheap lead that cannot be contacted or qualified is not a winning outcome.
- Example: two ads each produce a DKK 200 CPL. If Ad A yields 20% qualified leads and Ad B yields 60%, the qualified-lead costs are DKK 1,000 and DKK 333. Message and expectation alignment may explain the difference.

## B2B and high-ticket services

Several stakeholders may occupy different awareness states at the same time: a user is Product Aware, a CFO is Problem Aware and compliance only needs risk documented. Modular ads, cases and sales assets should answer each stakeholder's missing belief.
